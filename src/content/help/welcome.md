# Welcome to SkillAI

SkillAI is your team's internal recruiting portal. It does one job well: rank candidates against open roles fast, then keep them on file so you never re-sift the same CVs twice.

## What you can do here

- Upload CVs (PDF, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT, MD) and let AI parse them into structured profiles.
- Create job roles with requirements, language needs, location preferences and budget.
- Get every candidate scored against any role across four dimensions, with reasoning you can read.
- Search a growing archive of past candidates with semantic search ("find me senior backend engineers comfortable with Polish").
- Generate interview packs (questions + optional code challenges) tailored to each candidate-role pair.
- Upload interview transcripts and get an AI second opinion on how the conversation went.
- Export candidate profiles, role briefs and shortlists as PDFs — internal-detailed or customer-sanitised.
- Send candidates to a customer with a per-role share-link the customer responds through — feedback flows back to your dashboard, no email chasing. See [Sending candidates to a customer](/dashboard/help/roles-customer-submissions).
- Generate AI-personalised, multilingual welcome letters for shortlisted candidates. See [Welcome letter PDF for candidates](/dashboard/help/candidates-welcome-letter).

## How the pieces fit together

> **Roles** describe a job. **Candidates** describe a person. **Scores** connect the two. **Customers** group roles by client. **Agencies** track where candidates came from.

Most of your day will start on the **Dashboard** (open roles, recent uploads, top candidates, next interviews) and then move into a **role detail page** to work the shortlist.

## Things to know up front

- **Your data stays here.** SkillAI is self-hosted. Candidate CVs, notes and transcripts never leave your infrastructure.
- **AI is an assistant, not a judge.** Scores include reasoning, but the call to interview, offer or reject is yours.
- **Tenants are isolated.** You only ever see your team's roles, candidates and agencies — even if other teams use the same instance.
- **Roles don't auto-archive.** If a role's cut-off date passes you'll see a red EXPIRED badge, but it stays open until you archive it. Recruiters keep control of the lifecycle.

If something on this page is unclear or wrong, tell your admin — these articles are versioned and updated as the product changes.
