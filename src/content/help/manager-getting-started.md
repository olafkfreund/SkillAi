# Hiring manager: what you'll see when you log in

Welcome. SkillAI is the tool your recruiting team uses to score, rank, and shortlist candidates against the roles they're filling for you. This page walks you through what the hiring manager experience looks like from the moment you sign in.

## Your dashboard

When you log in you land on a focused dashboard, not a full recruiter cockpit. You'll see:

- **Roles awaiting your decision** — every role where the recruiter has sent a shortlist for your approval is at the top.
- **Recently decided roles** — what you've already actioned, in case you need to revisit your reasoning or comments.
- **Upcoming interviews** — any interview slots that involve you as the panel member.

You won't see the full archive of every candidate the agency has uploaded, every internal cost field, or the recruiter's note threads. The interface is deliberately narrowed to "what needs your attention."

## What you can see on a candidate

When you open a shortlisted candidate, you get the parts of the profile that matter for a hire decision:

- **Overall score** and the four dimension scores: technical skills, experience level, cultural / role fit, and communication.
- **AI reasoning** — the structured explanation of why each dimension was scored the way it was. Click any dimension to read the full rationale.
- **Candidate summary** — the AI-generated headline profile.
- **CV** — the cleaned-up structured CV (skills, experience, education).
- **Interview pack** (if generated) — questions and code tests the recruiter has prepared.
- **Interview transcript analysis** (if uploaded) — how the candidate scored against the role in their actual interview.

You will **not** see day rates, margin, or internal recruiter notes. Those are scrubbed from the manager view by design — your job is to judge fit, not to negotiate the commercial terms.

### Shareable notes

You will see notes the recruiter has explicitly marked **shareable**. The default for any new note is **private** (recruiter-only) — recruiters opt-in per note when they want managers to see it. This is a deliberate safety: notes often contain recruiter shorthand or commercially sensitive comments that aren't meant for the hiring panel.

If you expect a note to be visible and it isn't, the recruiter hasn't toggled the shareable flag yet. Ask them; they can flip it in seconds without rewriting the note.

## What you do here

Three actions, mostly:

1. **Approve** a candidate — they move forward in the process.
2. **Reject** a candidate, with a comment explaining why.
3. **Request more info** by leaving a comment for the recruiter without making a decision yet.

See [Approving and rejecting candidates](/dashboard/help/manager-approving-candidates) for the full workflow.

## Roles you're not assigned to

You only see roles where a recruiter has explicitly added you as a hiring manager. If you expect to see a role and it's missing, ping the recruiter — they need to assign you on the role detail page before the role appears in your queue.
