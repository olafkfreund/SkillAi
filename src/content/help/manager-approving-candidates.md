# Approving and rejecting candidates

When a recruiter sends a shortlist for your approval, the affected role moves to the top of your dashboard with a "Awaiting your decision" pill. This article walks through what to do from there.

## Open the shortlist

Click the role. You'll see the shortlisted candidates listed in score order — highest overall score first. Each row shows the candidate's name, overall score, and the four dimension scores (skills, experience, role fit, communication).

Click any candidate name to open the full candidate detail view.

## Read before you decide

You're being asked to make a hire-or-no decision, so spend a minute on each candidate. The view gives you:

- The **overall score** — a single 0–100 number, but don't stop there.
- **Per-dimension breakdowns** — click any dimension to expand the full AI reasoning. The reasoning is the most important thing on the page; the score is just the headline.
- The **candidate summary** — a short narrative profile.
- The **CV** — full structured profile (skills, experience, education).
- The **interview pack and transcript analysis** if the recruiter has run them.

> The recruiter often hides candidates that scored well overall but flagged on a dimension that matters specifically to your team. If a candidate is in your shortlist, the recruiter has already filtered for you — your job is to judge fit, not to re-rank.

## Approve

Click **Approve** on the candidate detail page. You'll be prompted for an optional comment — leave one if you want to flag something to the recruiter ("loved the data engineering experience, let's interview", "good — schedule the technical round"). Comments aren't mandatory but they help the recruiter prioritise.

The candidate's status changes to **approved**. The recruiter sees the decision immediately on their side, with your comment attached.

## Reject

Click **Reject**. A comment is **required** for a rejection — be specific. "Not enough cloud experience" is more useful than "not a fit." The recruiter uses your reasoning to refine future shortlists for this role and to feed back to the agency.

## Request more info

If you're not ready to decide — maybe you want the recruiter to dig deeper, schedule a screening call, or get a code sample — leave a comment without clicking Approve or Reject. The candidate stays in your queue and the recruiter sees your question.

## Bulk approve / bulk reject

If a shortlist has several candidates and you want to action them together, tick the checkbox next to each candidate and use the bulk approve / bulk reject bar at the top of the list. Comments still apply — a single comment is attached to all selected candidates in that action.

## Changing your mind

Approvals and rejections aren't immutable. Re-open the candidate, click the new decision, and add a comment explaining the reversal. The activity log tracks both decisions so the audit trail is intact.

## What happens after you approve

The recruiter takes the approved candidate forward — interview scheduling, offer, contract. You'll typically see them again in the **Upcoming interviews** widget if you're on the panel.
