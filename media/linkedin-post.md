## Why this is written the way it is

- **First line is a confession, not a claim.** LinkedIn hooks that start with "I" + mistake/overreach outperform product-forward openers in the SaaS category.
- **Specific numbers** (Next.js 16, Postgres 17, Claude Sonnet 4.6, four dimensions, 97 / 85 / 90 / 88) make the post feel built by a human who actually shipped the thing, not an intern writing marketing copy.
- **No em-dashes used as rhetorical pauses.** No "not just X, but Y". No "leverage / unlock / delve / seamless / robust". No tricolons. No "in today's fast-paced world". These are the 2026 AI-copy tells.
- **Closes with a question** about the reader's experience, not a CTA about the product. Comment-fishing works on LinkedIn because comments weigh more than likes in the algorithm.
- **3 hashtags, not 10.** Current LinkedIn guidance is that 4+ hashtags signals "spray and pray" and reduces reach.
